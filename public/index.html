How to play
  1. Each round shows a chemistry category, like “Name a halogen or a noble gas”.
  2. Whoever holds the flask types an answer. A correct, unused answer passes the flask to the next player.
  3. The fuse starts at 15 seconds and gets 1 second shorter with each pass, down to 6.
  4. Wrong or repeated answers just shake the flask, so keep trying!
  5. If the timer runs out, your flask boils over and you lose a life. The last chemist standing wins.

Names, symbols and formulas all count. Case and spacing don’t matter, and one typo is forgiven on longer words.
